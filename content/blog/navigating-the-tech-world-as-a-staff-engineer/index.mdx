> Important to say that the work of a staff engineer is not only on tech, but mostly on communication and "_convincing people that what you want to do is worth doing_", it's more a community management role than an engineering role.
>
> Mention the difficulties in being a new staff engineer because it's expected that you have a lot of domain knowledge in a company, even though you just joined it
>
> The importance of always asking for feedback and delegating work
> Keeping a document like What's going on at work and Brag Document, you will be evaluated not on the work you done yourself but on the work you successfully convinced others to do
>
> How is it like to have to go in different domains, areas and things like that, having to talk to teams and squeeze work in their sprints.
