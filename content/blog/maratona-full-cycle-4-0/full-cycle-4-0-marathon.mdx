I had the immense pleasure of being invited by Wesley to participate in the **Full Cycle 4.0 Marathon**!

The goal of this marathon is to show that it's possible to be a complete developer, from development to deployment and monitoring of the application!

In this talk of over 2 hours, we discuss cloud in general, its differences and how we can get the most out of them through publishing with containers using **Docker** and **Azure Container Registry**. We also used **Azure Container Instances**, **App Services**, went through **Azure Functions** and the so famous **Azure Kubernetes Service** to show that you can deploy your application effortlessly!
