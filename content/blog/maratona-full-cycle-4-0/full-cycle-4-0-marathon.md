I had the great pleasure of being invited by Wesley to participate in the **Full Cycle 4.0 Marathon**!

The goal of this marathon is to show that it's possible to be a complete developer, going from development to deploying and monitoring the application!

In this talk spanning over 2 hours, we discuss clouds in general, their differences, and how we can make the most of them through publishing with containers using **Docker** and **Azure Container Registry**. We also used **Azure Container Instances**, **App Services**, went through **Azure Functions**, and the famous **Azure Kubernetes Service** to show that you can deploy your application effortlessly!
