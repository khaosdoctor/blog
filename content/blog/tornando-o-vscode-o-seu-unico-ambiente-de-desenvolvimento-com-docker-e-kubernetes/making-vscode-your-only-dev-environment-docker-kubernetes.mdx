On October 3rd, I spoke at **BrazilJS.live()**, a completely online event organized by the well-known BrazilJS team.

That day, I decided to be bolder and give you a completely hands-on talk: how to turn our favorite editor, [**VSCode**](https://code.visualstudio.com/?WT.mc_id=personal-blog-ludossan), into the only development environment you need for **[Azure](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/), [Docker](https://docs.microsoft.com/dotnet/architecture/microservices/container-docker-introduction/docker-defined?WT.mc_id=personal-blog-ludossan), and [Kubernetes](https://docs.microsoft.com/azure/aks/?WT.mc_id=personal-blog-ludossan)**.

In this roughly 30-minute talk, I open my [VSCode](https://code.visualstudio.com/?WT.mc_id=personal-blog-ludossan) and show you the amazing extensions for [Docker](https://docs.microsoft.com/dotnet/architecture/microservices/container-docker-introduction/docker-defined?WT.mc_id=personal-blog-ludossan) and [Kubernetes](https://docs.microsoft.com/azure/aks/?WT.mc_id=personal-blog-ludossan). They let you manage any [Kubernetes](https://docs.microsoft.com/azure/aks/?WT.mc_id=personal-blog-ludossan) cluster straight from inside the editor. Plus, you can work with [Docker](https://docs.microsoft.com/dotnet/architecture/microservices/container-docker-introduction/docker-defined?WT.mc_id=personal-blog-ludossan) itself directly from [Code](https://code.visualstudio.com/?WT.mc_id=personal-blog-ludossan)!
