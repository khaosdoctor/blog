I had the great pleasure of being invited to join the amazing **#FalaDev** podcast by RocketSeat, together with Wesley Willians to talk about **Microservices**!

In this podcast we discuss how to understand microservices, how they work, what the classic use cases are, how we can improve our architectures, and how we can be much more efficient in terms of cost and project delivery using microservices!
