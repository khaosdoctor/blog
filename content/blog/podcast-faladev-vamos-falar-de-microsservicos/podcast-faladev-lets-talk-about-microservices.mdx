I got the pleasure of joining the amazing **#FalaDev** podcast, from RocketSeat, with Wesley Willians to talk about **Microservices**!

In this one, we covered how to understand microservices, how they work, classic use cases, how to improve your architecture, and how to get way better efficiency in both costs and shipping projects with microservices!
