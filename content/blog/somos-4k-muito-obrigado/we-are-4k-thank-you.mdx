I'd like to thank everyone who made this milestone possible! We're 4,000 people on Twitter who love technology and enjoy chatting and discovering more and more content!

![](./image_o-2.png "Thank you so much!")

When I started writing around 2016, I didn't expect to attract anyone. I didn't even imagine that one day I'd reach so many people interested in my content. Now, working full time with content and learning more and more to share knowledge with everyone, I understand this is truly the career I want to pursue!

## Will there be cake?

To celebrate this milestone, I'm doing a **giveaway**! For every thousand followers I'll raffle off a **Code House book** of your choice, meaning I'll raffle off 4 **physical** books to 4 people plus a **pack of 5 random stickers**! And one more **pack of 10 stickers** for another 4 lucky winners!

![](./image-2.png)

To enter is super simple, you just need to **be one of the newsletter subscribers**! Here's how:

1. In the bottom right corner of the screen there's a "Subscribe to Newsletter" button - just click it!
2. Enter your name and email
3. Click "Send"

That's it! If you're already a subscriber, you don't need to do anything else!

## How does it work?

The giveaway will happen on **January 15, 2021**, so everyone will have time to sign up for the list and receive emails! You can subscribe to the newsletter right up until the day of the giveaway!

The raffle results will be posted on my [Twitter](https://twitter.com/_staticvoid) and on [all my other social networks](https://lsantos.dev), as well as in a special newsletter. Once the winners are announced, I'll send a personal email to each winner asking which book they'd like and their delivery address!

If you won the sticker pack instead of books, I'll reach out the same way to get your delivery address!

Once the books are shipped, I'll contact you again with the tracking codes and updates :D

## And if I didn't win :(

No worries! Here **everyone wins**! All newsletter subscribers will get surprise discount codes to make learning even more accessible!

## Will there be more?

Of course! With each milestone of 1,000 new followers I'll do something special so we can celebrate our community's growth! Each time I'll try to secure new partnerships and giveaways!

If you're part of or know someone who could help make the giveaways even better, just reach out to me through my [email](mailto:lhs.santoss@gmail.com)!

## Thank you!

I'd like to thank everyone who's followed me from the start until now. This blog, all my content, everything I do would be nothing without people to discuss and read my ideas. So:

THANK YOU SO MUCH!
