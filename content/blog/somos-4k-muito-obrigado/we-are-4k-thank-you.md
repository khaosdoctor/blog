![](./image_o-2.png "Thank you so much!")

When I started writing, around 2016, I didn't expect to attract anyone. I couldn't even imagine reaching so many people interested in my content. Now, working full-time with content and constantly learning to share knowledge with everyone, I understand this is the career I want to pursue!

## Will there be cake?

To celebrate this milestone, I'm running a **giveaway**! For every thousand followers, I'll raffle a **Código de Casa book** of your choice, so I'll be giving away 4 **physical** books to 4 people plus a **pack of 5 random stickers**! Plus another **pack of 10 stickers** for 4 more people!

![](./image-2.png)

To participate is simple, you just need to **be subscribed to the newsletter**! Here's how:

1. In the bottom right corner of the screen there's a "Subscribe to Newsletter" button, just click it!
2. Enter your name and email
3. Click "Send"

That's it! If you're already a subscriber, you don't need to do anything else!

## How does it work?

The giveaway will happen on **January 15, 2021**, giving everyone time to sign up and receive emails! You can subscribe to the newsletter up until the giveaway day!

The results will be posted on my [Twitter](https://twitter.com/_staticvoid) and [all my other social media](https://lsantos.dev), plus in a special newsletter. Once winners are announced, I'll send a personal email to each asking which book they want and their address for delivery!

If you won stickers instead of books, I'll reach out the same way to get your delivery address!

Once the books are sent, I'll contact you again with tracking codes and updates :D

## What if I didn't win :(

No worries! Here **everyone wins**! Everyone on the newsletter will receive surprise discount codes to make learning even easier!

## Will there be more?

Of course! Each time we hit a milestone of 1,000 new followers, I'll do something to celebrate our community's growth! I'll keep trying to secure new partnerships and giveaways!

If you're part of this or know someone who could help make the giveaways even better! Just reach out to me via [email](mailto:lhs.santoss@gmail.com)!
