> This stems from the an internal planning doc situation where a colleague made a choice by himself to use Redis streams without telling anyone else, now the project is 3 weeks in and full of memory leaks because he also chose to use AI to write in a language he didn't know.
>
> This article is about owning your decisions and standing by them, or letting them go. It's about making a choice and knowing it will affect way more than your immediate surroundings, a good example I can use is to compare critical systems where tech decisions can impact lives and decides who live or die
