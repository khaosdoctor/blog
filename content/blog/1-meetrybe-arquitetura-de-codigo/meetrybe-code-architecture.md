Today I took part in **Meetrybe**, the first online event by [**Trybe**](https://betrybe.com) along with a bunch of heavyweight people like: [Elton Minetto](https://twitter.com/eminetto), [Rogério Munhoz](https://twitter.com/roziscoding) and [Igor Halfeld](https://twitter.com/igorhalfeld).

After outstanding talks by Elton and [Ana Cardoso](https://www.youtube.com/redirect?redir_token=QUFFLUhqa05peURIMk9MZkRPN2dKZVU5MmlGRnJ3QkxtQXxBQ3Jtc0tra1VSd1hHLWRpWkthYmpmY1JtRk83b3o3MUFLTUdPdF9lalA0QWdRSFFWdU9DMWRVYy1LZkZaNE1OLTA2ckNqM2hlSnlGUUVZdldFbDZiazdpS2lVM0p6c2pPVTBEbnR2WnpOM0dRYVRWVFJMUmpKcw%3D%3D&q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.linkedin.com%2Fin%2Fcardosoanacp%2F&v=3fmUp3PICLo&event=video_description), we put together a Q&A circle about software architecture. We cleared up a lot of really cool questions and even came up with iconic lines like:

> Every legacy system was once a hype

It was a very relaxed conversation! Take a look at how it went!
