> This is a blog post about how uninteresting tech has become. Everything is just a variation of the same product: AI. The old race to have the better computer, to be the bleeding edge software is gone, there's nothing new, the only thing we have now is a bunch of big corporations that are running after each others tails trying to copy one another out of FOMO.
> This leads to tech shambles like [this](https://heatherburns.tech/2025/10/22/democracy-and-the-open-internet-die-in-daylight/) where a, once respectful newspaper, is now succumbing to the ads for free articles, or [this](https://manuelmoreale.com/thoughts/look-another-ai-browser) other excellent article about how everything is just the same thing over and over again, just branded as the next great thing.
> Important to mention: How Apple, once praised for its UX and design, is now just as bad as any other phone, sometimes even worse.
