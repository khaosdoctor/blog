> Also about Anton Holmgren and related to the Own your choices blog post. This is about asking the question whether a SWE needs to jump between different topics and different companies to be called a Senior SWE, otherwise if you only work in a single place your whole life, than you never have life experience from other places that can do things differently, but at the same time, if you keep jumping from company to company you will never deeply learn anything.
