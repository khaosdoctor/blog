Fonts served from this folder, and what each one obliges us to do.

Nothing here is applied to the site yet. Every file exists so /theme-lab/ can render
it at a real size. Anything that does not get picked should be deleted, licence note
and all.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Redistribution is free, including in a commercial product, as long as the font file
itself is not sold on its own and any derivative font keeps the OFL and does not use
the reserved name. Serving a woff2 from a website is exactly what it is for. No
attribution is required on the page, though the licence text must travel with the
font file.

Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Different obligations, and the reason this face is called out separately.
Attribution is required, and ShareAlike applies to anything derived from the font.
Serving it as a webfont is fine, but the credit below has to stay reachable from the
site, and a modified version of the font would have to be released under the same
licence.

  PxPlusIBMVGA8-9x16.woff       "Web437 IBM VGA 9x16", from The Oldschool PC Font
                                Resource v2.2 by VileR.
                                https://int10h.org/oldschool-pc-fonts/
                                Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

                                This is a bitmap face traced to outlines. It is only
                                crisp at whole multiples of 16px: 16, 32, 48. At 17px
                                or 20px it blurs, which /theme-lab/ demonstrates.
